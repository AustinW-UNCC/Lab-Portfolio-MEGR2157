Reading and Writing - 

After reading the article that was attached on the assignment, I have a better understanding of the types of questions that I should start asking myself when looking to design products that make things better. According to design thinking principles, asking thoughtful and strategic questions is a critical investigative phase that prevents teams from prematurely rushing into superficial solutions. Instead of treating inquiries as interruptions in fast-paced environments, effective designers use questions as a tool to uncover underlying user needs, grasp complex contexts, and identify genuine opportunities. By acting like investigators who dig past initial assumptions, designers ensure they are solving the right problems rather than wasting valuable resources on the wrong ones. Ultimately, framing the right questions fosters cross-functional collaboration, sparks creativity, and guides stakeholders toward making informed decisions that result in truly valuable products.

Problem Statement - 

The purpose of my design is to make it easier to open metal water bottles with tight lids. 

I noticed the problem with my sister and mom and how they always have to ask me to open the bottles for them when they go to wash them. Most of the reason is that the lids are very thin and have a small surface area to grab onto, which makes it much harder to open them. They also have to tighten the lids well or else it will risk the liquid spilling out if the bottle ever falls over. 

This product is mainly being designed for people who struggle with similar issues of opening their own bottles. This mainly relates to people who own the Hydrojug bottles because they come with small lids which are very hard to open if the lid is ever put on tight. 

Example of the Hydrojug bottles that I am referring to  -

My approach for my design is to see how I could reduce the amount of force required to open the lids while also making a small object that would not take up lots of room or space so that it could be thrown into a purse or pocket if you wanted to travel with it. Making a design that was also small enough was the hardest part of the design process. 

Design - 

I started by making the handle part of the design. This part is 5 X 1 X 0.5 inches; this can be changed based on the user if they have smaller or larger hands. 

<img width="229" height="532" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a3615a15-1edf-45c9-a1f3-f22decc9cf39" />

The next step was to design the part of the wrench that actually would fit inside the lid of the water bottle. I used calipers to measure the necessary dimensions. This part was also extruded 0.5 inches; making the full height of the wrench 1". I determined the allowances to be 0.02 inches less than what the actual bottle was because I wanted to guarantee that the 3D printer would have no issues with making it fit. This is allowed because the tolerances for a part like this can be much more loose than other types of parts. There is not a massive need to make super tight tolerances for this wrench.

Tolerances - 

I mentioned my reasons for tolerances up above the second picture of the design process. This part allowed for tolerances to be lower than many of the previous parts that I have designed. It actually turned out to be a much tighter fit than I expected and I am very glad that I gave extra room for tolerance.

 Lessons Learned - 

The main lesson that I learned from this process was how to identify a fixable problem and determine that it is an achievable goal prior to ever starting the actual design process. There were a few ideas I had that would have been much more complicated and may have not even turned out proper had I continued on that path. However, this project gave me an idea from the beginning and the entire process was thought out prior to even opening SolidWorks. That being said, I also learned that it is fun to come up with ideas about improvements for the future before even finishing the original design. An example of this is the hole that I added to the handle of the part. This could be useful to many people in the future if this were to become an actual product on the open market. 

This project took about 4-5 hours in total from start to finish because I ran through many different design options before settling on this one.
