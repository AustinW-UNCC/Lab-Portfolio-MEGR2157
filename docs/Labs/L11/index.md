# A11 – Snap Fit Resin Print

Objective - Design and 3D print a small artifact composed of two or more parts that snap together using the Form 4 SLA printer.
The goal is to design, test, and document a functional snap fit while understanding how material properties, tolerances, and printer settings affect assembly performance and durability.

You may design any small artifact, provided that:

It demonstrates a working snap fit between two or more components.

It is not just a plain test block; the design must be creative and/or useful (e.g., a small enclosure, clip, latch, hinge, or functional connector).

2–3 student projects will share one build plate.

Research and Principles - My design is a cantilever-based lever. The main principle that I decided to pay attention to on this build is how the snap fits can be used to hold things together other than just attaching to themselves. 

There were no sketches or drawings as I just started to make this part in CAD without a pretensive drawing.
